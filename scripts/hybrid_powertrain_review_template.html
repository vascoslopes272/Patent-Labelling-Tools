In the domain: the patent states that the propulsors can be driven by electric motors fed from on-board batteries — as the only source, or as one of the stated options, or in an electric mode.

Out (No + ElectricSimilar): the aircraft needs a combustion engine to fly — the engine turns a propeller/fan, or the engine makes the electricity (series hybrid, turbo-electric, range extender with no battery-only flight). A fuel cell does not count as electric.

Unknown: the patent never says where the energy comes from (stays in, by the existing rule).   Hybrid → Yes: "hybrid" only appears in background text; the aircraft is electric.
