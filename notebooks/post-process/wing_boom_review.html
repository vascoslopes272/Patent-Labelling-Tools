Wing or boom? — rotors near the wing Rule: a member on a wing that carries a rotor is a boom when it carries rotors both ahead of and behind the wing, reaches past the leading or trailing edge by more than the local wing chord, or carries a tail surface. Otherwise it is a nacelle / pylon and the rotor goes on the wing card (LE, TE, Above, Below). The analysis counts both as wing-borne thrust, so this only makes the detail consistent.
